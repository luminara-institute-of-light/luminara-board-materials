Records Custody Rollout Plan

From the founding interim control to the standard institutional practice.

Why a rollout

The corporation is building its records practice while it operates. Two things are true at once: the founding record must be protected now, before systems exist, and the practice the corporation settles into should match what peer institutions do rather than a procedure invented for this moment. This plan holds the interim control in place and names the standard it converts to.

Stage one · Interim control, in effect on adoption

Every record added to the Corporate Records drive is printed within thirty calendar days of each quarter's end and deposited in the hardcopy set held in a locked safe at the principal office. The Chair verifies the deposit against the digital record and signs the Quarterly Records Deposit and Verification Certificate.

The control is deliberately broad because the corporation's records are young, scattered across personal accounts and prior-era folders, and not yet consolidated. Breadth substitutes for infrastructure that does not exist yet.

Stage two · Consolidation

StepCompletion condition
Corporate Records drive establishedShared drive in the organization's Workspace, not a personal account, with access limited to Secretary, Chair, and the Treasurer.
Founding set depositedArticles, certificate, filings, bylaws, minutes, resolutions, policies, and the determination letter when it arrives, all present as the drive's first content.
Prior-era material migratedRecords held in personal accounts, prior-era folders, and predecessor identities are copied in or formally excluded.
Backups runningThree copies, two media, one off site, verified once.
Access logWho holds access, reviewed when anyone joins or leaves.

Deadline. Every step above is complete by September 1, 2027, the date the corporation retires its legacy references to The Light School. The transition to standard practice and the transition to a single name conclude together.

Stage three · Standard practice

When stage two is complete, the board narrows the control to the institutional standard and records the change in the minutes.

ElementStandard practice
Hardcopy setPermanent-class records only: articles and amendments, bylaws and amendments, minutes, resolutions and written consents, adopted policies, the determination letter, and state filings. This is the corporation's minute book.
Everything elseHeld digitally under the retention schedule, with access controls and backups. Not printed.
Deposit rhythmPermanent records added to the minute book as they are executed, rather than swept quarterly.
VerificationThe Secretary confirms the minute book's completeness at each annual meeting; the Chair countersigns the annual confirmation.
ReviewRetention policy and custody practice reviewed annually with the conflict of interest disclosures.

Conversion

The interim control stays in force until the board resolves that stage two is complete. The plan does not convert on its own; conversion requires that resolution. That resolution is the moment the quarterly certificate is retired and the annual confirmation replaces it. Until then, the broad deposit continues, and no record is lost while the systems are still being built.
