The Pioneer Path

Every Pioneer has a place to begin, a way to contribute, and a visible next step. This document defines the entry roles, how to take one, and where each leads.

Version 1.0 · Owner: Branding & Outreach · Intake: the Secretary · Adopted under Resolution 9

The ladder

How to take a role

Offer through the member profile or to the Secretary at intake. Each offer is logged with the role, the home division or Bridge Group, and a start date; the roster records who holds what. One role at a time to start; a Pioneer may change roles at any intake.

Tracking

The Secretary keeps the roster: name, role, home, start date, and next step. Each division or Bridge Group lead confirms contributions quarterly. The roster is the source for ratifying leads and for the record of work the institute publishes.
