Letter of Gratitude

Dear ,

Thank you for your gift of . Before anything else, we want you to know what it means to the people doing this work.

An institute at the beginning of its life runs on two things: the labor of the people who build it, and the faith of those who believe the building matters. Your generosity is that faith made tangible. It arrived at a moment when every gift shapes not just what we do, but what we become, and it will be remembered that you were here early, when belief cost the most and meant the most.

Luminara was founded so that others may grow, as they give light in turn.

We hold every gift as a trust. Yours goes directly to the work it was given to further, and you are warmly invited to see that work for yourself, at whatever depth you wish. Our door is open to you, and it will stay open.

Your contribution is received through our fiscal sponsor, the Virtual World Society, whose formal acknowledgment will reach you separately.

From all of us who carry this work: thank you for choosing to carry it with us.

With Light,

For Luminara Institute of Light
