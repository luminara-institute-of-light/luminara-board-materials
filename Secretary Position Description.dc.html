Secretary

Authority: Bylaws Sections 5.11, 6.1, 6.9 · Article X · RCW 24.03A.585, .590

Purpose

The Secretary is the keeper of the corporation's memory: its minutes, notices, records, and governing documents. When anyone asks what the corporation decided and when, the Secretary's records are the answer.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure accurate minutes are kept for board meetings and for committees exercising board authority, recording attendance, actions, votes, recusals, and conflicts disclosed.
  • Give and record the notices the bylaws require for meetings and actions.
  • Maintain the corporate records: articles, bylaws, minutes, resolutions, policies, filings, and adopted instruments, in the "Corporate Records" drive and the hardcopy set.
  • Administer the quarterly records deposit; receive signed disclosures, acceptances, and certifications for filing.
  • Certify adopted documents and record adoption dates upon them.

Limits

The Secretary may delegate ministerial recordkeeping while retaining oversight responsibility, and may not simultaneously serve as Chair or President.

Acceptance. I accept the office of Secretary of Luminara Institute of Light, acknowledge receipt of the bylaws and the Conflict of Interest Policy, and agree to perform the duties of the office as described here and in the bylaws, subject to the standards of conduct of RCW 24.03A.
