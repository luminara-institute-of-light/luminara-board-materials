Resolve every bylaws bracket to a recommendation. Work from BYLAWS-OPEN-ITEMS; attach a one-line rationale each. Decide the membership recommendation: board-governed, "member" as community title, no voting class.

Day 1 · The organizational meeting · the root gap closes here

If the board vote already happened: retrieve, sign, and file the minutes: confirm they cover bylaws adoption and officer elections; anything missed becomes a ratification item. If not: convene the two governors and run the agenda (remote is valid; email notice is valid, per RCW 24.03A defaults).
GOV + SEC
Adopt bylaws; elect the board; elect officers. Chair signs the bylaws, secretary attests, adoption date recorded.
GOV → BRD
Pass the remaining pre-written resolutions (policies, fiscal year, bank, programs, leads, HeartBeam owner, intake, retention).
BRD
Minutes finalized and signed within 24 hours; deposited as the first item in the corporate-records drive. Conflict-of-interest disclosure forms collected from all nine.
