Expense Reimbursement Policy

The policy the bylaws call for in Sections 4.11 and 6.11: how directors, officers, and volunteers are reimbursed for reasonable expenses incurred in the corporation's service.

Who is covered

Directors, officers, committee members, and volunteers acting on the corporation's behalf, and employees if and when the corporation has them.

What is reimbursable

Reasonable and necessary expenses actually incurred in carrying out corporate business: travel and mileage, program materials and supplies, printing, postage, filing fees, software and services purchased for corporate use, and meeting costs. Any single expense over $250 requires approval before it is incurred, on the corporation's pre-approval form.

What is not reimbursable

  • Alcohol, fines, penalties, and personal items.
  • Vehicle mileage. The corporation does not reimburse mileage; volunteers may be able to deduct qualifying charitable mileage on their own tax returns.
  • Per diem allowances. Meals are reimbursed at actual documented cost only.
  • Travel or meals for family members or companions.
  • Any payment that would constitute compensation for board service, which the bylaws prohibit.
  • Expenses of a transaction in which the person holds an undisclosed conflict of interest.

Substantiation

Requests are submitted on the corporation's reimbursement form within sixty days of the expense, with receipts for any expense of $25 or more and a statement of the business purpose. Advances, when granted, are settled promptly, and any excess returned within one hundred twenty days of the expense. Requests substantiated and settled on these terms are intended to qualify as an accountable plan under federal tax rules, so reimbursements are not income to the recipient.

Approval

The Treasurer reviews and approves requests. The Treasurer's own requests are approved by the Chair, and the Chair's by the Treasurer: no person approves a reimbursement to themselves. Approved requests are paid promptly under the corporation's financial controls, and the record of each request and approval is retained under the Document Retention and Destruction Policy.

Adoption

Adopted as board policy by resolution of the Board of Directors on , recorded in the minutes. The board may amend this policy by resolution; amounts stated here may be revised the same way.

Called for by Bylaws Sections 4.11 and 6.11. Administered by the Treasurer. Filed with the corporate records.
