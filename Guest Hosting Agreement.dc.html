1 · The engagement

The guest will present or teach as described above. The engagement is a one-time appearance, not employment, and creates no ongoing relationship, agency, or authority to act for the Institute.

2 · Covered costs

None (invitation only)  Travel  Travel, lodging, and meals at actual documented cost

Covered items are booked directly by the Institute where possible. Costs the guest advances are repaid on itemized request with original receipts, within sixty days of the engagement. Coverage applies to the guest only; companion costs are not covered. No fee or honorarium is payable under this agreement unless a separate written addendum, approved by the board's process, provides one.

3 · Recording and use

The Institute records many of its programs. The guest:  consents to recording and to the Institute's use of the recording, with attribution, in its programs, archive, and public materials  consents to recording for the Institute's internal archive only  does not consent to recording

The guest retains ownership of their own materials and presentation content; the Institute's use of any recording is per the consent marked above, and either party may revisit that consent in writing.

4 · Cancellation

Either party may cancel with notice as far ahead as circumstances allow. If the engagement is canceled, the Institute covers only non-refundable costs already incurred under Section 2, and neither party owes more.

5 · General

The guest is responsible for their own conduct, insurance, and taxes. This agreement is governed by Washington law, is the whole agreement for this engagement, and may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.
