INTERNAL · FOR THE BRIDGE · WORKING LIST

Prepared by Alley Till for Branding & Outreach · alley.till@luminara.institute · begun September 2, 2026

Method

Each board position description cites the authorities its duties rest on. Every citation either points at an instrument that exists or reveals one that must be created. This list walks each position, pulls every reference, and records what it found. Statute citations were verified against the published RCW text at the Washington State Legislature on September 2, 2026.

Statute verification

All ten distinct citations verified accurate: RCW 24.03A.120 (organization of corporations), .495 (standards of conduct, directors), .580 (procedure for remote meetings), .585 (officers), .590 (standards of conduct, officers), .630 (indemnification and advance for expenses), .906 (distribution of assets on dissolution), .908 (corporations holding charitable property), chapter 1.80 RCW (Uniform Electronic Transactions Act), chapter 19.09 RCW (charitable solicitations).

One nuance for ruling: RCW 24.03A.585(1) states officers "consist of a president, secretary, and treasurer"; it names the office, and no subsection permits title substitution. Resolution 3 leaves the President's office expressly vacant while the Chair performs its functions. Hardened alternative: declare the Chair is the corporation's president within the meaning of RCW 24.03A.585, serving under the title Chair of the Board. Queued for Alley's ruling and the attorney-review pass already on the adoption checklist.
