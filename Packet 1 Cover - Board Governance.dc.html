Board Governance Packet

Organizational Meeting of the Board

This packet contains the instruments the board considers, adopts, and signs at the organizational meeting. Each document is final as presented; changes are made by motion at the meeting and recorded in the minutes.

1Agenda
Order of business and the resolutions before the board.
2Board Briefing Memorandum
Matters carrying a judgment call, with recommendations. Read before the vote.
3Presiding Officer Memorandum
Why Resolution 2 seats the Chair in the office of president; a Major Action under Bylaws Section 5.9.
4Resolutions
Fourteen resolutions, in the order acted on: board seated, officers seated, then every adoption. With the Secretary's certification.
5Bylaws
Adopted under Resolution 3.
6Minutes Template
Completed by the Secretary during the meeting; the founding governance record.
7Conflict of Interest Policy
Adopted under Resolution 4.
8Annual Conflict of Interest Disclosure Statement
Signed at the table by each director and officer.
9Financial Controls Policy
Adopted under Resolution 6; sets the $5,000 dual-authorization threshold.
10Expense Reimbursement Policy
Adopted under Resolution 6, with its Pre-Approval Request form and the Reimbursement Authorities reference.
11Banking Resolution
Bank-facing instrument confirming the existing account and naming the signers; presented with the Certificate of Incumbency and the Certificate of the Secretary.
12Gift Acceptance Policy
Adopted under Resolution 14, with its Gift Acceptance Record form.
13Remote Meeting Procedures
Adopted under Resolution 14.
14Document Retention and Destruction Policy
Adopted under Resolution 10.
15Document Execution and Signing Protocol
Adopted under Resolution 10.
16Records Compliance Forms
Working forms for the retention policy: deposit certificate, destruction log, annual review record.
17Founding Charter
Adopted under Resolution 13; published in place of the working CODEX.
18Branding Style Sheet
Adopted under Resolution 12 as the shared source for all materials and the authority on names and spelling.
19Records Custody Rollout Plan
Companion to the retention policy; the path from the interim deposit to standard practice.
20Pioneer Path
Exhibit to Resolution 9; published following the meeting.
21Board Position Descriptions
Chair, Vice Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, Director: scope of each office as seated under Resolutions 1 and 2.
22Governance Guide and Public Contacts
Plain-language guide to the bylaws, and who holds what; both take effect with the adoptions.
23Meeting templates
Meeting Notice, Regular Meeting Agenda, Regular Meeting Minutes, Treasurer's Report Template: the forms for every meeting after this one.

Minutes of the meeting are prepared by the Secretary during the meeting and filed with this packet in the corporation's shared drive designated "Corporate Records."
