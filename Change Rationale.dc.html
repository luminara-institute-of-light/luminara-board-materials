The position in one paragraph

Almost nothing in this packet was invented. The structure follows the Chair's own Codex-to-Structure presentation, already shown to the community. The legal particulars follow the filed record and current Washington statute. The identity decisions follow the founding materials and Drew Stone's brand work, which is untouched. Where a judgment call was made, it is named as one, the Board Briefing Memorandum argues it, and the board can redirect it by motion today.

Drew Stone's colors and typography. The founding language: the signature question, the motto, and the mission opener are quoted verbatim. And the board slate, which is presented as publicly stated, with elections today making it the board of record.

If challenged

The order of authority behind this packet: the filed public record first, current statute second, the Chair's structure presentation third, the brand steward's materials for identity, and the group lead's judgment only where none of those speak. Every judgment call is disclosed in the Board Briefing Memorandum rather than buried, and each one can be redirected by motion today. That is the defense: the packet does not ask the board to trust the drafter; it shows its sources.
