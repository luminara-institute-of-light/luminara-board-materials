Remote Meeting Procedures

How the board meets and votes by remote communication, under Bylaws Section 5.4 and RCW 24.03A.580. The board conducts most of its business this way; these procedures make it sound.

Presence and participation

A director joining by conference telephone, videoconference, or other communication through which all participants can hear one another or otherwise participate contemporaneously is present in person for all purposes, including quorum and voting. A director whose connection drops is absent until it is restored, and the minutes note the interval.

Identity

Each participant is identified at the start, by voice or video, so the Secretary can confirm who is present and that only directors vote. A participant who cannot be identified is not counted toward quorum.

Voting

Votes are taken by roll call or by a clear affirmative response from each director, not by silence. On any Major Action, on any contested matter, and whenever the Chair or a director requests it, the vote is taken by roll call and each director's vote is recorded by name. There is no proxy voting.

Documents and simultaneous access

Any document to be voted on is distributed to all directors before or during the meeting so that each can review the same text. A resolution is not adopted from a document some directors cannot see.

Disruption and fairness

If a technical failure prevents a director from participating contemporaneously, the Chair pauses material business until participation is restored or the board decides to proceed, and the decision is recorded. The meeting is conducted so that every director can hear the discussion and be heard before a vote.

Notice and recording

The meeting notice states the platform and how to connect. Any recording of a meeting follows the board's practice and is disclosed to participants; the written minutes, not the recording, are the official record.

Adoption

Adopted as board procedure by resolution of the Board of Directors on , recorded in the minutes; reviewed with the annual policy review.
