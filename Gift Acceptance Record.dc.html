Kept under the Gift Acceptance Policy; reported in the Treasurer's Report; retained with the financial records. The Letter of Gratitude and Letter of Declination are sent from their companion templates, and a copy of each sent letter files with this record. For a declined gift, record the specific reason here, never in the letter. The fiscal-sponsorship sentence in the Letter of Gratitude retires if and when the IRS issues a favorable determination letter; from that date the letter must add the written-acknowledgment statement federal law requires for gifts of $250 or more, including whether any goods or services were provided in exchange.
