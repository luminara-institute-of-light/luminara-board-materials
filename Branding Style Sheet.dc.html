Application rules

Board and legal documentsLegal name in full. Quotes from filed instruments match the record exactly; a restyled quote is a misquote.
Outbound mailAll correspondence from @luminara.institute. Signature blocks carry the settled public name.
Decks and templatesNo legacy template ships after rollout.
Division materialsEach division uses its settled name and mark; its pages on the website reflect the current structure.
Every pageCarries the Luminara logo and the name: the full legal name on business documents; Luminara alone on materials and outreach. Absence flags counterfeit or sloppy work.
Never color aloneAny meaning a color carries is also carried by a word, shape, or pattern.

Custody

Upon adoption, this sheet is deposited to the org Workspace as the single shared source. On any approved change, the sheet is updated the same day and the website follows within three business days.
