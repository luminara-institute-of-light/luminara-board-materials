Organizational Meeting of the Board

Held , Washington, on , 2026,
at

The governors of record of Luminara Institute of Light named in the Articles of Incorporation filed with the Washington Secretary of State effective May 7, 2026, convened the organizational meeting of the corporation pursuant to RCW 24.03A. Notice was given to, or waived in writing by, each person entitled to it. A quorum being present, the following resolutions were considered and adopted.

Resolution 1 · Election of the board of directors

RESOLVED, that the following persons are elected directors of the corporation, to serve in accordance with the bylaws until their successors are elected and qualified:

Thomas A. Furness III · Daniel Oestreich · Michele Atwater · Trond Nilsen · Mark Billinghurst · Dave Lorenzini · Marty Perlmutter · Drew Stone · Stacy Whittle

Resolution 2 · Election of officers

RESOLVED, that the following persons are elected officers of the corporation, to serve at the pleasure of the board in accordance with the bylaws:

ChairThomas A. Furness III
Vice ChairDaniel Oestreich
SecretaryMichele Atwater
TreasurerTrond Nilsen

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chair of the Board is and serves as the president of the corporation within the meaning of RCW 24.03A.585, holding that office under the title Chair of the Board, and that the office of president is thereby filled for all purposes of the Washington Nonprofit Corporation Act.

Resolution 3 · Adoption of bylaws

RESOLVED, that the Bylaws of Luminara Institute of Light presented to this meeting are adopted as the bylaws of the corporation; that the Secretary is directed to record the adopted text, note this date of adoption upon it, and file it with the corporate records; and that the Chair shall sign and the Secretary attest the adopted text.

Resolution 4 · Conflict of interest policy

RESOLVED, that the Conflict of Interest Policy presented to this meeting is adopted; and that each director and officer shall complete an annual disclosure statement, beginning with disclosures collected by the Secretary within thirty days of this meeting.

Resolution 5 · Fiscal year

RESOLVED, that the fiscal year of the corporation shall end on December 31 of each year.

Resolution 6 · Depository and financial controls

RESOLVED, that the depository account already opened in the name of the corporation is ratified and confirmed as the corporation's depository account; that the Chair shall deliver the account records, credentials, and the IRS EIN confirmation letter to the Treasurer and the Secretary for the corporate record; that the Treasurer is authorized to open such further accounts as the board approves by resolution; that the Financial Controls Policy and the Expense Reimbursement Policy presented to this meeting are adopted; that any single disbursement above the dual-authorization threshold set by the Financial Controls Policy, being $5,000 or, once an annual budget is adopted, one percent of budgeted annual expenses, whichever is greater, requires the authorization of the Treasurer and the Chair; that the Treasurer shall reconcile the accounts monthly and a director who is not an account signer shall review the reconciliations quarterly; and that a budget-versus-actual statement shall stand as an item on the agenda of every regular board meeting.

Resolution 7 · Divisions and leads

RESOLVED, that the corporation's program structure consists of six divisions: Research; Technology, AI & Information Systems; Experience Architecture & World Building; Lumina Lingua; The Light School; and Community Service; that the board may adopt updated names and groupings as the 2027 plan is settled; that interim leads shall be appointed by the Chair, subject to ratification at the next regular board meeting, each for a term of one year; and that Project HeartBeam is assigned to Research as its single accountable home, with the others as contributors.

Resolution 8 · Bridge Groups

RESOLVED, that the Bridge, the corporation’s organizing core group, begun in 2021 as the Virtual World Society’s Futures Subcommittee, continues as the corporation’s infrastructure, now comprising four Bridge Groups: Governance, Finance & Fundraising, Internal Communications, and Branding & Outreach, which carry the corporation’s enabling functions under the oversight of the board of directors elected at this meeting.

Resolution 9 · Community intake

RESOLVED, that the intake path presently administered by the Secretary is adopted as the corporation's process for welcoming community members, the community title "Pioneer" being its settled form; and that the corporation shall publish the Pioneer path, including entry roles and progression, on its website.

Resolution 10 · Corporate records and retention

RESOLVED, that the corporation's records of formation, bylaws, minutes, policies, and determinations shall be kept permanently in the corporation's shared drive designated "Corporate Records," maintained by the Secretary; and that the Document Retention and Destruction Policy and the Document Execution and Signing Protocol presented to this meeting are adopted.

Resolution 11 · Fiscal sponsorship and federal exemption

RESOLVED, that the Chair and the Treasurer are authorized to negotiate and execute a written fiscal sponsorship agreement with the Virtual World Society defining the sponsored fund, administrative fee, variance power, and termination terms; that the officers are directed to prepare and file IRS Form 1023 seeking recognition of exemption under section 501(c)(3), and to register with the Washington Secretary of State Charities Program as required; and that unless and until federal recognition is received, all solicitation materials shall state that contributions are received through the Virtual World Society.

Resolution 12 · Public language and style

RESOLVED, that the corporation adopts inquiry framing as the house style for public copy concerning research programs, favoring phrases such as "designed to support" and "exploring whether"; that all outcome claims shall be routed through one reviewer designated by the Chair before publication; and that the one-page style reference of settled names, marks, and palette is adopted as the shared source for all materials.

Resolution 13 · Founding Charter

RESOLVED, that the Founding Charter of Luminara Institute of Light presented to this meeting is adopted as the corporation’s public statement of purpose, commitments, and structure; that the Secretary shall record the date of adoption upon it and file it with the corporate records; and that it shall be published in place of the working CODEX, which continues as an internal working document.

Resolution 14 · Board policies and Advisory Council

RESOLVED, that the Gift Acceptance Policy and the Remote Meeting Procedures presented to this meeting are adopted as board policies; and that the Secretary shall record the date of adoption upon each and file it with the corporate records.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that an Advisory Council is established under Section 7.4 of the bylaws, without vote or Board authority, its members to be appointed by resolution of the Board.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.

Certification
