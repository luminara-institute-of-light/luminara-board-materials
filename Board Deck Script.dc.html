Board Deck Script

One block per slide: why the slide is there, what is on screen, why it matters, and the cue into the next. No lines to memorize; speak from the point in your own words.

FOR THE PRESENTER

Prepared by Alley Till for Branding & Outreach · alley.till@luminara.institute

01Title

WHY THIS SLIDE IS HERE  Opens with the founding question and tagline so the review starts from identity, not criticism. The board hears who Luminara is before hearing what needs work.

ON SCREEN  the founding question, the adopted tagline, and the institute's name.

NEXT SLIDE  advance for the honest summary of where things stand today.

02Where we stand

WHY THIS SLIDE IS HERE  The honest baseline. Names the three fronts (Purpose, Pioneers, Luminara) and states each one's gap plainly, so every recommendation that follows lands against a stated problem.

ON SCREEN  the current state on the three fronts the review examined: Purpose, Pioneers, Luminara.

WHY IT MATTERS  this is a finding, not a criticism; the review concluded the raw material is strong and that discipline is the remaining work.

NEXT SLIDE  'Twelve recommendations follow, four per front. The first front is Purpose.'

03Purpose · findings

WHY THIS SLIDE IS HERE  The evidence for front one. Shows the board why purpose language needs discipline before asking them to adopt anything about it.

ON SCREEN  the four Purpose findings.

WHY IT MATTERS  purpose language is the first surface funders, partners, and newcomers judge.

NEXT SLIDE  'Here is what the review recommends we do about it.'

04Purpose · recommendations

WHY THIS SLIDE IS HERE  The four Purpose moves. Several are in today's packet (Charter, language standard), so this slide connects the review directly to resolutions being voted on.

ON SCREEN  recommendations 1 through 4, the Purpose front. The banner shows the settled pairing: the question as signature with the tagline beneath it.

IF ASKED  the Founding Charter and the public language standard are both in today's board packet for adoption.

NEXT SLIDE  'The second front is our supporters.'

05Supporters · findings

WHY THIS SLIDE IS HERE  The evidence for front two. Establishes that Pioneers exist but lack a visible path, which is the problem Resolution 9 and the Pioneer Path exhibit solve.

ON SCREEN  the four Supporters findings.

WHY IT MATTERS  people stay where they can see their own next step.

NEXT SLIDE  'Here is what we build.'

06Supporters · recommendations

WHY THIS SLIDE IS HERE  The four Pioneer moves. Ties the community's energy to concrete adoptions: the path, the roster, the ladder from first task to contributor.

ON SCREEN  recommendations 5 through 8, the Supporters front.

IF ASKED  the Pioneer Path document is in today's packet and is adopted under Resolution 9; its entry roles are presented as representative examples that each home shapes at intake.

NEXT SLIDE  'The third front is identity.'

07Identity · findings

WHY THIS SLIDE IS HERE  The evidence for front three. Shows the identity spread (two eras, multiple names) that makes the one-name-one-mark standard necessary.

ON SCREEN  the three Identity findings.

WHY IT MATTERS  identity is trust infrastructure; a mixed identity reads as an unfinished organization to exactly the funders and partners we want.

NEXT SLIDE  'Four final recommendations.'

08Identity · recommendations

WHY THIS SLIDE IS HERE  The four Luminara moves. Lands the branding standard and the website pass, both anchored in today's Branding Style Sheet adoption.

ON SCREEN  recommendations 9 through 12, the Identity front, under the settled motto Grow and Give Light.

NEXT SLIDE  'Last slide: how this review was built, and where the depth lives.'

09Method &amp; companions

WHY THIS SLIDE IS HERE  Closes on method: how the review was built and against whom Luminara was measured. Gives the findings their credibility and invites questions.

ON SCREEN  the method and the companion documents; this is the close.
