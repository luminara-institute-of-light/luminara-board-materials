Letter of Declination

Dear ,

Thank you for your generous offer of , and for the confidence in our mission it represents. That confidence means a great deal to an institute at our stage, and we do not take it lightly.

After careful review of our mission and policies, we are unable to accept this gift. Our responsibility is to accept only what we can honor fully: every restriction kept, every obligation met, and every gift used in a way that protects both the donor's intent and the institute's mission. In this case we cannot meet that standard, and accepting anyway would serve neither of us.

We would welcome a conversation about other ways your generosity could support this work, whether an unrestricted gift, a different program, or a future moment when we are built to receive what you offered. This is not a closed door.

Thank you again for thinking of us. Institutions are built by people willing to give, and we hope to earn your support in a form we can fully honor.

With Light,

For Luminara Institute of Light
