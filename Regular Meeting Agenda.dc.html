Regular Meeting Agenda

The standing order of business for a regular meeting of the Board of Directors. The Chair sets the agenda with the Secretary; items are added under New Business.

Meeting date: 
Time: 
Place / platform: 
  1. Call to order and quorum. The Chair opens the meeting; the Secretary confirms a quorum is present.
  2. Approval of the agenda. Including any items added since notice, subject to the Major-Action limit.
  3. Approval of prior minutes. The minutes of the last meeting are corrected as needed and approved.
  4. Chair's report.
  5. Treasurer's report. The standing financial report, received and filed with these minutes.
  6. Committee and division reports. As scheduled; Bridge Groups and program divisions as called.
  7. Consent agenda. Routine items adopted together in one motion unless a director asks to remove one.
  8. Old business. Matters held over from a prior meeting.
  9. New business. Resolutions and matters for decision. A Major Action appears here only if it was stated in the notice.
  10. Conflicts and disclosures. Any conflict arising on an agenda item is disclosed and handled before the vote.
  11. Executive session, if needed. Confidential personnel, legal, financial, or conflict matters.
  12. Next meeting and adjournment.
